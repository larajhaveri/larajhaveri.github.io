# About [Your name]

I’ve worked at an early-stage startup, helped shape a grocery marketplace across global markets, and am now a second-year MBA at Berkeley Haas.
{: .page-intro}

## Finding the work I want to do

Before Haas, I spent four years at Delivery Hero working across product and commercial strategy for its grocery marketplace in 18 markets across APAC, Europe, and Turkey. I built the commercial logic for why a feature should exist, what it could do for GMV and retention, and how to phase it with the product team.

I was the bridge between what the market needed and what the product team built: writing business cases, defining metrics, and feeding back what was and wasn’t moving the numbers. That upstream problem definition is the part of product I’ve found I’m good at.

Before that, I was employee number ten at an HR-tech startup in Singapore. I ran a pre-Series A fundraise and took on what was effectively a chief-of-staff role. The early-stage experience taught me to operate without much in place; Delivery Hero taught me how to execute at scale.

This summer, I was at BlueRobins, an early-stage marketplace connecting K–12 students with mentors. I led a team of four and built the mentor onboarding and lifecycle system.

That combination of startup scrappiness and enterprise execution is what I want to bring to product decisions with commercial ownership behind them.
